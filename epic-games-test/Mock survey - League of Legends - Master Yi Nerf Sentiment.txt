Sample Size
* Rule of thumb: 500 responses per segment ensures complex analysis that needs statistical significance. (minimum is 100)
   * Yi players = 500
   * Non-Yi players = 500
   * Mode (Ranked/normal) = 1000
   * Tier (Iron-Silver, Gold-Platinum, Emerald-Diamond, Master+) = 2000
   * Experience as Yi (Main/not main) = 1000


Considering:
* The response amount needed for each segment (and the minimum)
* The population Distribution
* The loss after cleaning: 15% of the responses
* Sending method: Email (12% response rate)


Calculation: (Always based on the smallest segment)


Target: 150 clean Master+ responses
Master+ population: 3%


Step 1: Clean responses needed (to get 150 responses, with a 3% population size)
        150 ÷ 0.03 = 5,000 clean total


Step 2: Add 15% cleaning buffer
        5,000 ÷ 0.85 = 5,882 raw responses


Step 3: Email send volume (at 6% response rate benchmark)
        5,882 ÷ 0.06 = ~98,000 emails


Total minimum: 100k emails sent - 6% response rate, 6000 responses




Representativeness Ensurance


1. Understand Population distribution
2. Ensure proper sample size
3. Set up quotas for each population segment (region, platform, gender, etc.)
4. Mid-Survey Check
   1. Send reminders if necessary (Focus on the segment with a deficit)
