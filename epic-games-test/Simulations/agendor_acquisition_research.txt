================================================================================
RESEARCH CASE STUDY: ACQUISITION CHANNEL OPTIMIZATION
Agendor (Brazilian B2B CRM)
================================================================================

BUSINESS CONTEXT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Company: Agendor — CRM for small/medium sales teams in Brazil
Challenge: Marketing needed to optimize acquisition spend across channels
My Role: Data Analyst supporting Marketing team

STAKEHOLDER REQUEST:
"We're spending across Google Ads, LinkedIn, organic content, and referrals.
We don't know which channel brings the best customers or what messaging 
resonates. Help us understand where to double down."

================================================================================
STAGE 1: REQUEST — UNDERSTANDING REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

INTAKE QUESTIONS & ANSWERS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: "What decision will this research inform?"
A: "Budget allocation for next quarter — shift spend to highest-value channels."
→ Need: ROI-focused analysis, not just volume

Q: "Who will use these results?"
A: "Marketing Director for budget decisions, Content team for messaging."
→ Need: Executive summary + actionable messaging insights

Q: "What segments need separate analysis?"
A: "Company size (1-5, 6-20, 21-50 employees) and industry if possible."
→ Need: ~200 per company size segment = 600 minimum responses

Q: "Is this one-time or ongoing?"
A: "One-time for quarterly planning, but might become a tracker."
→ Deliverable: Static report + presentation, dashboard optional

RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

RQ1: Which acquisition channel delivers the highest-quality customers?
RQ2: What messaging/value proposition resonates most by segment?
RQ3: What is the ideal timing and touchpoint sequence for conversion?

================================================================================
STAGE 2: DESIGN — METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

APPROACH:
Survey customers who converted in the last 90 days, combining self-reported
discovery channel with behavioral data from product database.

TARGET POPULATION:
- Users who signed up in last 12 months
- Paid customers OR active trial users (7+ days in trial)
- Logged in at least 3x in last 30 days

SAMPLE SIZE CALCULATION (500-per-segment rule):
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Segments: 3 company sizes (1-5, 6-20, 21-50 employees)
Smallest segment: 21-50 employees (~20% of customer base)

Calculation:
  500 / 0.20 = 2,500 clean responses needed (ideal)
  Target 1,000+ clean to match large-scale research standards
  
Available population:
  - Paid customers (12 months): ~4,500
  - Active trial users (12 months): ~12,000
  - Total eligible: ~16,500 users
  
Expected yield:
  - Email response rate: 25% → 4,125 raw responses
  - Clean rate: 85% → 3,506 clean responses ✓
  
→ Large-scale research: 3,500+ clean responses
→ All segments will have n > 500 for robust subgroup analysis

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
METHODOLOGY NOTE:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Why rating scales instead of MaxDiff for messaging?
- Prioritized response rate over methodological complexity
- Rating matrix gives directional preference with lower cognitive load
- MaxDiff would add 2-3 minutes, reducing completion rate
- For this decision (budget allocation), directional insights were sufficient

Trade-off acknowledged: Rating scales can suffer from scale bias (everyone 
rates high). Mitigated by looking at RELATIVE rankings, not absolute scores.

FIRMOGRAPHICS NOTE: Company size and industry pulled from database 
(not self-reported) to reduce survey length and improve accuracy.

STAGE 4: DISTRIBUTE
================================================================================

TOOL: SurveyMonkey (email distribution)

DISTRIBUTION STRATEGY:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Channel: Email with personalized survey link (SurveyMonkey collector)
Timing: Sent on Tuesday 10am (highest open rates for B2B)
Incentive: Entry into R$500 iFood voucher raffle
Subject line: "Quick question about your Agendor experience (2 min)"

Why email instead of in-app (Intercom)?
- Survey has 10 questions (~5 min) — too long for in-app interruption
- Email allows respondent to choose when to complete
- Higher quality responses (dedicated attention vs. multitasking)
- Trade-off: Lower response rate (20-25% vs 40% in-app)

TARGETING QUERY (PostgreSQL):
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SELECT 
    u.user_id,
    u.email,
    u.company_size,
    u.signup_date,
    u.acquisition_source,  -- UTM captured at signup
    u.subscription_status,
    COUNT(d.deal_id) as deals_logged
FROM users u
LEFT JOIN deals d ON u.user_id = d.user_id
WHERE 
    u.signup_date >= CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '12 months'
    AND (
        u.subscription_status = 'paid'
        OR (u.subscription_status = 'trial' AND u.trial_days_active >= 7)
    )
    AND u.last_login >= CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '30 days'
GROUP BY u.user_id, u.email, u.company_size, u.signup_date, 
         u.acquisition_source, u.subscription_status
HAVING COUNT(d.deal_id) >= 1 OR u.subscription_status = 'trial';

-- Result: 16,482 eligible users

REPRESENTATIVENESS CHECK (before sending):
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SELECT 
    company_size,
    COUNT(*) as count,
    ROUND(COUNT(*) * 100.0 / SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER(), 1) as pct
FROM survey_targets
GROUP BY company_size;

-- Result:
-- 1-5 employees:   8,076 (49%)
-- 6-20 employees:  5,109 (31%)
-- 21-50 employees: 3,297 (20%)
